# Atelier Domain Model

Glossary and canonical domain concepts for Phuket Atelier Makeup.

## Language

**Consultation Inquiry**:
A direct contact request submitted by a prospective client seeking bridal or private makeup styling.
_Avoid_: Lead, ticket, order

**Client WeChat Handle**:
The WeChat username or identifier provided by a client in the inquiry form so the stylist can initiate communication.
_Avoid_: User account, personal account

**Stylist**:
The professional private makeup and hair artist providing one-on-one styling services in Phuket.
_Avoid_: Vendor, provider, operator
